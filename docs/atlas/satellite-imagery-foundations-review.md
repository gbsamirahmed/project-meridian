## What Meridian actually implements

**MapTiler belongs to the public experimental React frontend**, not the current private Capacitor/TypeScript prototype. The private map has OpenFreeMap, Mapterhorn hillshade and a development-only Riffelhorn contour overlay; no satellite selector, MapTiler key, Weather or map inspector. No private files or existing application behaviour changed here.

The public reference path is [provider configuration](../../src/atlas/map/satelliteProvider.ts) → [raster lifecycle](../../src/atlas/map/satelliteLayer.ts) → [AtlasMap](../../src/atlas/map/AtlasMap.ts). `VITE_MAPTILER_KEY` is optional and browser-visible, sourced from ignored environment configuration; its value was not printed or committed. The owner confirmed the existing Free plan and at least400 available request units, then approved a bounded live comparison. Account restrictions, exact endpoint billing weight and spending settings were not independently inspected. No new key, account or subscription was introduced.

Strengths: lazy TileJSON, per-map promise deduplication/metadata cache, mandatory attribution,512 px raster source, preserved original non-symbol layer visibility, labels retained, style reload restoration, explicit unavailable/error/degraded status and conventional-map fallback. Opacity1/linear filtering/fade180 ms are portrayal, not radiometric calibration. Satellite deliberately suppresses IGOR hillshade; independent elevation/weather layers remain separate. The [5 October metadata receipt](appearance-baseline-metadata.json) returned JPEG, z0–22 and no scheme/tile-size field; XYZ is the renderer default. This is historical metadata, not a refreshed mosaic revision.

Limitations: no imagery dates/local-GSD UI or per-location contributor record; no application request/byte/spend cap; metadata fetch lacks a timeout/abort signal; only non-empty tile templates are validated. A synthetic test confirms an arbitrary non-empty host is accepted, without contacting it. Browser/MapLibre caches are not an offline package. Source-loaded status alone should not be treated as evidence that every image tile succeeded. The basemap state keeps hillshade suppressed during photographic fallback: a policy question, not a newly demonstrated rendering defect. No working satellite implementation is replaced or protected reference source altered.

## Regional options

**Switzerland — preferred regional foundation.** [SWISSIMAGE](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/orthoimage-swissimage-10) is aircraft-derived RGB orthophotography: advertised10 cm plains/main valleys,25 cm Alps, three-year cycle; LV95/EPSG:2056 COG downloads and documented [XYZ](https://docs.geo.admin.ch/visualize-data/xyz.html)/[WMTS](https://docs.geo.admin.ch/visualize-data/wmts.html) services. A tile's year denotes the contributor covering at least70%, not every pixel's date; exact mosaic seamline/date breakdown is unavailable. Preserve special Planet notices for large-lake contributions where applicable. [Custom Swiss OGD terms](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/terms-of-use-free-geodata-and-geoservices) permit commercial use, processing and distribution with ©swisstopo/source acknowledgement; they are not a CC licence. [FSDI terms](https://www.geo.admin.ch/en/general-terms-of-use-fsdi) require fair use, permit restrictions and provide best effort rather than a service warranty. Download products, not a WMTS scrape, for offline packages. Live service URL shape is `https://wmts.geo.admin.ch/1.0.0/ch.swisstopo.swissimage/default/current/3857/{z}/{x}/{y}.jpeg`; matrix limits must be checked before a trial. No service imagery request occurred here.

**Scotland/UK — no equivalent open national sub-metre foundation was verified in this bounded review.** [OS imagery](https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/maps-imagery) advertises25 cm GB orthophotography, but [OS OpenData](https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/open-data) does not establish an open imagery licence. [APGB](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/natural-england-licence-agreement-aerial-photography-and-height-data) access is entitlement/partner/contractor specific; public-sector access does not transfer to Meridian. [Bluesky's product sheet](https://bluesky-world.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bluesky-Data-Sheets-2026_GB_RGB.pdf) advertises12.5 cm coverage and selected5 cm urban capture, including Scottish urban areas; actual area/date/licence/delivery price needs a quote. Neither is accessed or purchased. These are aerial products, not proof of current imagery at every mountain/coast.

[Environment Agency vertical photography](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/4921f8a1-d47e-458b-873b-2a489b1c8165/vertical-aerial-photography) is project-specific RGB/NIR/RGBN open data under the catalogue's OGL, not seamless Scottish coverage; exact tile CRS, resolution/date and third-party exclusions need inspection. Historical [Scottish aerial archives](https://ckan.publishing.service.gov.uk/dataset/royal-commission-on-the-ancient-and-historical-monuments-of-scotland-aerial-photographs) are valuable evidence but Crown copyright/viewer access does not grant an orthophoto basemap licence. Prefer licensed global imagery for provisional Scotland coverage while investigating a specific authoritative regional acquisition; do not relabel publicly viewable photographs as reusable tiles.

Technical request-rate ceilings are separate from cost caps. The [Mapbox raster API](https://docs.mapbox.com/api/maps/raster-tiles/) documents100,000 requests/minute and429 handling, not a spending ceiling. Swiss [2025 fair-use changes](https://www.geo.admin.ch/fr/nouvelles-limites-fair-use) announce doubled limits; their rendered table was not available reliably here, so the exact current WMTS limit/limiting identity remains a preflight check rather than a guessed entitlement. No uniform MapTiler/Esri requests-per-second guarantee or account-specific rate limit was verified. Preserve refusal/429 handling; never cycle keys or endpoints to bypass limits.

Illustrative monthly assumptions:1,000 users×3 visits×60 tile HTTP requests =180,000 requests;5,000×4×50 =1,000,000. With unused whole allowances and charge weight1, indicative MapTiler Flex totals are$30/$105, Esri tile charges$0/$0, Mapbox raster charges$0/$62.50. **These are arithmetic scenarios, not recommendations or spending authorisation.** If MapTiler weight4 applies, totals become$63/$555. Metadata, other services, cache misses, retries, misuse, FX/tax and hosting change the result; logs/account analytics must establish billable units first.

Before public use: owner-approved budget/exposure ceiling, restricted/revocable client-visible keys, provider-side caps verified for the actual plan, bounded retries and requests, safe suspension/error messaging, and a credible delivery funding model. Client counters alone cannot bound a copied key or direct API abuse. Requests expose IP, viewed tile coordinates, client metadata and account key/token; imagery dates are not user locations, but request paths reveal viewed areas. No GPS/analytics is needed for this research. A proxy must not be introduced where terms prohibit it. High-resolution storage/offline downloads need their own lawful, measured budget; no global cost extrapolation from4 km².

RGB images supply no vertical heights; contour heights remain LN02. This flat/no-terrain viewer is not the previous pitched3D research renderer or the private application's Mapterhorn map.

## Decision and single next experiment

Keep MapTiler as the configured **public-reference** global candidate; no provider ranking or private satellite replacement is supported by current visual evidence. Prioritise Swiss OGD for rights-clear regional detail. Esri is the strongest later global comparator because its attribution/source metadata route is documented; Mapbox remains conditional on binding rights/account review. UK public-viewer imagery and public-sector entitlements are not a free reusable foundation.

**Single next experiment:** a bounded Riffelhorn SWISSIMAGE-versus-global imagery comparison in the private flat map, at the two matched ground scales, retaining source-specific notices and testing contour/path/label readability with photographic hillshade off by default. Use existing local Swiss products; a new global trial requires renewed scope/quota approval. No new architecture or worldwide data preparation. The five-location trial supports retaining the existing global candidate, but does not settle exact cartographic styling, local GSD/epoch metadata, offline entitlements, sustainable public delivery or physical-device performance.
