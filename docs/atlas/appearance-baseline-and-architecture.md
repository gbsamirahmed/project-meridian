## Programme boundary and meaning

**REGIONAL ELEVATION FOUNDATION = ESTABLISHED.** The [Terrain Hierarchy Contract](terrain-hierarchy-contract.md), [runtime slice](terrain-runtime-selection.md) and [real Welsh proof](tryfan-second-region-proof.md) establish metadata, registration, spatial/scale eligibility, regional pyramids, deterministic selection, parent fallback, source-family handoff, provenance and delivery. No further elevation benchmark is recommended. Reconciliation/morphology, vertical harmonisation, coarse handoff, hard support edges, close-camera clipping and true 3D remain unresolved; they do not block this programme and are not investigated here.

Terrain appearance means **the portrayal of the surface using identifiable observations, prepared appearance and rendering**, rather than a satellite URL or an assumed material texture:

| Domain | Meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geometry | Represented physical surface shape | TerrainProduct / TerrainHierarchy; visual geometry remains separate from analytical elevation |
| Source observations | Sensor observations, or an authoritative delivered imagery dataset when underlying frames are unavailable | Observation identity, spectral/radiometric meaning, acquisition geometry/time and limitations; a delivered mosaic is already processed |
| Processed appearance | Orthorectification, mosaicking, resampling, radiometric/topographic processing or reconstruction | Immutable AppearanceProduct with lineage and declared geometry dependencies |
| Cartographic / rendered appearance | Display transfer, colour grading, opacity, filtering, relief, lighting, fog and atmosphere | Renderer configuration; does not relabel the underlying imagery |

**MERIDIAN EVIDENCE** below is retained project evidence or this code audit, not a new visual experiment. **EXTERNAL EVIDENCE** is linked primary documentation/literature checked on this date. **RESEARCH HYPOTHESES / DIRECTIONS** label untested Atlas adaptations and future experiments. Established methods are not Meridian inventions.

## Retained appearance findings — MERIDIAN EVIDENCE

Read the [closed synthesis](../earth-lab/atlas-terrain-representation-synthesis.md), [012B](../earth-lab/riffelhorn-012b-mountain-reconstruction.md), [012F](../earth-lab/riffelhorn-012f-terrain-lighting.md), [012G](../earth-lab/riffelhorn-012g-projection-and-illumination.md), [Swiss acquisition record](riffelhorn-data-discovery.md) and [Tryfan observed-colour control](../earth-lab/tryfan-010-observed-natural-colour.md). None was rerun.

- Swiss RGB exposes paths, material boundaries and fine markings on ordinary ground. Finer supplied imagery in 012A exposed additional appearance with fixed geometry, but acquisition differences confounded a pure resolution comparison. Existing work supports useful regional appearance; it does **not** establish a new matched Atlas SWISSIMAGE-versus-MapTiler information-gain measurement. That comparison remains next.
- Retained four 2023 Swiss tiles cover LV95 `[2624000,1091000,2626000,1093000]`: 25 cm nominal native information on a 10 cm distributed grid. Complete XY imagery does not prove that vertical/occluded faces were observed. Orthophoto stretching was measured in 012G; nominal tangent footprint on the dark face had median 0.561 m and p95 13.38 m, versus 0.269/0.312 m ordinary-ground control. This is a view/geometry diagnostic, not optical MTF or true sensor visibility.
- 012F stronger geometric lighting improved some ridge/gully readability; photographed dark areas and opposed-light conflicts persisted. Cast-shadow mottling was not accepted. Those Unreal controls are not IGOR behaviour or an accepted Atlas lighting change.
- 012G's single conservative normalisation was **not accepted**, and causal ranking remained inconclusive. Sampled source values were not empty: pre-display probes showed amplified variation reaching the shader while final LDR conversion strongly suppressed it. Do not infer that all dark pixels are shadow, that no photographic signal exists, or that increasing gain recovers usable detail.
- Geometry curtains, projection loss, acquisition radiometry and display transfer remain separate. No intrinsic albedo, precise pixel-to-flight mapping, recovered cliff observation, new normals or multiview reconstruction was established. Acquisition-Sun reconstruction and visibility-aware texture selection remain literature-backed directions, not Meridian results. Historical Tryfan seasonal observations also demonstrate that acquisition time/classification matters independently of publication time.

## Appearance limitations and recoverability

The following is an architecture classification informed by the retained evidence and methods below. “Can help” is conditional, not a promise of reconstruction; none defines a scalar imagery-quality score.

| Problem | Missing / distorted information | One orthophoto, metadata and processing | Need for other observations / owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source information scale | Fine spatial signal absent or attenuated | GSD/resampling/MTF statements constrain claims; interpolation cannot restore independent detail | Finer observations for genuine gain; source/product |
| Orthographic / terrain projection | Foreshortened or stretched surface texture; displaced above-ground features | Geometry/UV audit can expose distortion; poses and surface model enable improved reprojection only where sampled | Other view often needed for steep faces; preparation, then renderer sampling |
| Occlusion | Surface never visible to the contributing observation | Visibility metadata/masks can identify lack of support; one mosaic cannot supply hidden evidence | Another observing view required, or explicitly synthetic fill; preparation |
| Acquisition illumination | Shadow/low exposure and mixed material/illumination | Time/Sun/terrain plus usable signal may support normalization; saturated/absent signal cannot be recovered by gain | Alternate illumination may be necessary; product processing |
| Radiometric variation / BRDF | Colour/brightness differences unrelated to material alone | Calibration, transfer, atmosphere and angular metadata constrain corrections; single RGB mosaic underdetermines reflectance | Multi-angle/calibrated observations for stronger physical claims; preparation |
| Temporal variation | Different snow, vegetation, water or land-cover states | Epoch/season/contributor masks allow explicit selection; change is not automatically error | Another epoch for desired state; source/product and selection policy |
| Mosaic seams | Inconsistent exposure, geometry, epoch or contributors | Overlap calibration, seam selection/blending may reduce visible seams, but can alter pixels and conceal change | Source overlap needed for accountable remosaicking; preparation |
| Geometry/texture misregistration | Imagery features misplaced on chosen surface | Exact geometry revision/CRS/poses aid diagnosis; correction needs support, not visual offset fitting | Better geometry/registration if warranted; preparation dependencies |
| Renderer lighting | Added relief competes with baked photographic lighting | Reproducible lighting ablations distinguish portrayal from imagery; cannot recover observations | Renderer; keep source identity intact |
| Display / generalisation | Filtering, aliasing, quantization or transfer suppresses existing signal | Declared colour transfer, pyramid/filtering and screen footprint audit can help | Representation preparation plus renderer; no new information from overzoom |

## Terrain concepts: reuse without a universal hierarchy

| Classification | Concepts / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Directly reusable semantics | Known/unknown knowledge, source identity, immutable prepared product/revision/assets/hashes, authority/rights/attribution, horizontal reference, lineage/evidence references, spatial coverage versus validity, common/regional role, deterministic ordering/fallback and honest unpinned services |
| Adaptable | Acquisition/change semantics, information ceiling, per-level support, coherent family parents, contributor mapping, processing/validation and representation identity. Imagery needs spectral/colour/visibility/time detail and appearance-specific tests; copying terrain interfaces wholesale would lose meaning. |
| Terrain-specific | Height kinds/geoid/vertical transformations, DTM/DSM surface semantics as imagery's value type, elevation encodings, broad/detail elevation blending, numerical height gradients and terrain morphology validation. Terrain protection/band distances are not imagery constants. Geometry's height semantics can appear on a referenced dependency. |
| Imagery-specific | Sensor/platform, bands/calibration/value units, GSD versus information, transfer/colour space, camera/strip footprints/poses, Sun/time basis, orthorectification, masks, mosaic seamlines, local epochs/contributors and radiometric/synthetic state |

**Decision B: separate `AppearanceHierarchy` and `TerrainHierarchy`, sharing small provenance/product primitives.** Geometry and appearance can select independently and have different missing-information/error models. A universal geospatial selector would prematurely conflate heights with radiometry; fully separate duplicate identity/licence systems would also be wasteful. The existing [metadata foundation](../../src/atlas/terrain/metadata/terrainMetadata.ts) supplies `Knowledge`, `AssetReference`, `ReferenceSystem`, `SpatialArea`/`SpatialSupport` and `RightsInformation` meanings. Reuse these meanings; a later implementation may extract only actually shared types into an Atlas-neutral module with compatibility exports/tests. No extraction, terrain-contract change or universal registry occurs now. `TerrainSource`, `TerrainProduct`, `TerrainDelivery`, height/temporal-specific structures and selector are not renamed or used as imagery objects.

## Proposed Appearance declarations — RESEARCH HYPOTHESES / DIRECTIONS

This is the minimum maintained proposal, not a frozen executable schema. Use IDs/references and explicit unknowns rather than duplicating records inside selections.

| Entity | Minimum responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `AppearanceSource` | Authority/dataset/observation or scoped delivered mosaic; release and acquisition separately; assets/rights; native CRS; sensor/platform/bands/value/calibration knowledge; source GSD and information statements; footprints and optional time/view/Sun evidence. A provider dataset may conceal its original frames: say so. |
| `AppearanceProduct` | Immutable prepared ID/revision + preparation/content manifest, or explicitly unpinned external service. Contributors, ordered operations/software/parameters, colour/band/encoding meaning, independent coverage/validity/masks, epochs, scale support, geometry dependencies, rights, evidence and limitations. |
| `AppearanceRepresentation` | Exact product/revision and form/level Atlas consumes. First form: tiled colour raster draped on selected terrain. Orthophoto/true-ortho/mosaic/corrected state describes the product, not a renderer URL. Future texture-atlas/multiview forms are reserved descriptive possibilities with unsupported adapters, not implementations. |
| `RegionalAppearanceFamily` / pyramid | Coherent declared representations from one appearance family, available lower-detail parents and per-level support/information ceiling. Parent operator, colour transfer and mask propagation explicit; no requirement to provide all levels. |
| `AppearanceHierarchy` | One common family, zero or more regionals; eligibility by declared support/scale, deterministic order, same-family parent fallback, explicit common handoff/unavailable. Independent of terrain selection; no viewport-aware engine required initially. |
| Appearance processing/selection policy | References actual preparation operations and optional prepared boundary products; selection/fallback and temporal preference separate from source facts. No automatic radiometric reconciliation or correction engine. |

Proposed chain: source → prepared product → representation/family → eligibility/selection → raster delivery adapter → **existing basemap/lifecycle**. Terrain selection supplies geometry separately. Weather and Traverse have no appearance dependencies. Current `SatelliteProviderConfig` remains a renderer/service boundary, not a replacement for these declarations.

### Value classes and provenance

Keep **product origin**, **processing operations** and **render modifications** orthogonal:

- **Source-derived appearance:** traceable resampling/projection of an identified source family. This includes previously processed authoritative mosaics; never label them raw sensor data.
- **Corrected / normalized appearance:** declared radiometric/topographic/illumination operations alter imagery values. May still be source-derived; preserve original assets and operation identity. RGB is not albedo and correction does not automatically produce reflectance.
- **Synthetic / reconstructed appearance:** inferred/inpainted/generated observations or estimated surface properties. Declare extent, method and evidence. Estimated albedo needs a physical model and limitations; unobserved face fill cannot masquerade as photography.
- **Render-only modification:** opacity/grade/relief/display/light/filter configuration alters portrayal, not product provenance; record it on captures/evaluation state.

Product-level lineage suffices for uniform single-source preparation. Spatial substitution/mosaic/correction/synthetic support requires polygons/masks or a reconstructible mapping when location-specific contributors or processing states vary. Require enough to identify local immediate product revisions and operations; use catalogue-only/unknown when upstream mapping is absent, never fabricate per-pixel flight provenance. Contributor interpolation weights are **not confidence, probability or accuracy**. Support, epoch/change and synthetic/correction masks have separate meanings.

Minimum provenance answers: contributor here; acquisition knowledge; processing; geometry revision used; illumination basis used; radiometric alteration state; observed-derived/synthetic origin; render-only state; rights. Missing upstream answers remain explicitly unknown. Validation separately records transfer fidelity, registration assessment, mask/lineage completeness, parent/colour consistency, clipping/radiometric changes, source-edge, visual/navigation checks and limitations; no universal quality score.

### Support, scale, time and illumination

**Support:** source coverage ≠ prepared coverage ≠ valid deliverable support ≠ informative surface observation. Cloud/shadow/snow/occlusion are classified conditions with observation/derived/inferred basis and uncertainty, not interchangeable nodata. They may be deliverable but unsuitable for a particular policy. Do not reject every dark pixel as missing. Per-level complete/partial/unknown/absent support must be explicit; imagery tiles' alpha/valid masks are not implicit Swiss terrain masks. Hard photographic handoff is acceptable in the initial proof with seams/change recorded; no closed stable ring or radiometric blend is required.

**Scale:** retain source GSD, known effective information/measurement statements, distributed pixel spacing, preparation resampling, delivery levels, same-family parents, overzoom, renderer texture filtering and screen-space footprint separately. Prefer coherent anti-aliased lower levels of the same appearance family, with declared colour-space/transfer and nodata handling. This is the useful terrain-pyramid analogy. Height-mean consistency metrics and source-family height jumps do not transfer to RGB. Parent/synthetic boundary validation instead tests colour, masks, aliasing, provenance and visual transitions.

**Time:** acquisition instant/date/range with precision/timezone and heterogeneous mosaic status; optional local epoch/season/change mapping; publication, processing and receipt dates separately. A year placeholder is not a flight instant; “latest” is not “current surface”. Snow/vegetation/glacier appearance change is a physical state, not a correction residual. Explicit temporal priority may select an observation; no universal newest-is-best rule.

**Illumination:** optional timestamp, Sun azimuth/elevation and coordinate/convention/basis; distinguish provider-observed metadata, ephemeris-derived angles and image-inferred illumination. Record spatial/temporal scope, uncertainty and direct-incidence/cast-shadow masks with their generating geometry. Deriving Sun from a possible flight does not prove that flight supplied a pixel. Unknown exposure/atmosphere/BRDF/transfer prevents physical reflectance claims. None of these fields is mandatory when unavailable.

### Geometry dependency and renderer boundary

Products that use geometry reference exact TerrainProduct ID/revision/content identity where available, role (orthorectification/topographic correction/shadow/projection), CRS/height transformation if relevant, method and knowledge limits. An upstream orthophoto may only expose a model name/grid; keep exact revision unknown rather than borrowing Atlas's current geometry identity. Dependency mismatch is assessable evidence, not automatic reprocessing or tightly coupled source selection. Runtime draping geometry is separate from geometry used to prepare the imagery.

Prepared operators own orthorectification, source-image selection, mosaicking/seams and radiometric/topographic correction. Renderer owns opacity, display transforms, filtering, fog and relief/lighting. Capture state references both. IGOR remains suppressed on satellite as current policy; the question of useful cartographic relief over photographs remains experimental, not an architecture invariant. No renderer modification here. User UI can later expose provider/epoch range/common-or-regional/processed state and a concise limitation, with details on demand; rights stay visible. Unknown dates must not become “current imagery”.

## Storage and delivery implications

At 0.1 m distributed RGB8, 1 km² has 100 million pixels, **300 MB uncompressed**; the 4 km² base is 1.2 GB. A full 2× colour pyramid adds about one third, ≈1.6 GB uncompressed before masks/aprons/tile overhead. The [SWISSIMAGE specification](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/WchyQCcLkyd9/Produktinfo_SWISSIMAGE10cm_DE.pdf) estimates ≈60 MB/km² compressed download; its product page estimates ≈55 MB/tile. Thus four source tiles are roughly 220–240 MB, an estimate, not a fresh download measurement. RGBN adds a band; float intermediates and immutable corrected variants multiply working/storage costs. Compression, coverage and chosen delivery ceiling make prepared size uncertain; do not extrapolate old Unreal texture resources into a CDN design.

For context, the same distributed RGB base has 18.75× the uncompressed bytes of a 0.5 m float32 height grid per unit area; this compares storage grids/encodings, not source information or every compressed prepared product. Source archive, prepared revision, derived variant and captures need distinct accounting under `meridian-data`. Git retains lightweight metadata/manifests/recipes/tests; no national download. A later delivery implementation can use revision-keyed object storage/CDN and local development subsets; infrastructure, costs and processing throughput are not chosen here. MapTiler's temporary end-user cache is not that archive.
