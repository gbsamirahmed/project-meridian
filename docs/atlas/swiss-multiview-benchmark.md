## Frozen screening — MERIDIAN EVIDENCE

`pre-discovery-freeze.json` was retained before detailed candidates (2026-10-06T00:16:24Z): 60 m square patches, median slope ≥50°, at least 25% of cells with orthographic stretch >2; a promising view must reach incidence ≤60° on ≥half those difficult cells, and its pair must differ by ≥15°. Incidence ≤60° bounds the normal-projection penalty at 2; this tests a physical support improvement, not accuracy. No weights/composite score, threshold tuning or image inspection.

Separate dimensions: steepness; geometry quality; observation count; camera completeness; direction diversity; incidence; visibility analysability; pixel access; temporal coherence; rights; experiment size. Prefer same acquisition, sampled LOS support, a minimal two-frame set, then deterministic coordinate order. Within a strip, the recorded pair rule chooses the largest median-incidence contrast satisfying the gate; ties use IDs. This is a transparent diagnostic choice, not a universal imagery-quality score.

Programmatic search: date/centre geography reduces the 26,601-row catalogue to August's 319 frames. Four paginated STAC responses recover all 319 footprints; API `limit=1000` still returns 100/page, so pagination is checked. Fourteen fixed east–west height-profile transects (7/cluster, 1 km separation, 101 points at 130 m spacing) provide 1,414 coarse samples. No smoothing offset. Only footprint-covered points with ≥2 observations enter coarse ranking; choose the three distinct 1 km tiles with greatest absolute coarse along-transect slope. Coarse one-dimensional slope is a discovery filter, not the final surface normal.

Each retained tile has a fixed 16×16 lattice of 60 m squares, 20 m edge clearance, no manual relocation. Screened **768 patches**, **80 steep qualifying patches**, **29 patches meeting the geometry gate** (13 /3 /13 by tile; each patch may admit multiple pairs). At all 900 native cell centres per patch, upward ENU normals use `(-dz/dE,-dz/dN,1)`; raster southward rows are accounted for. Orthographic stretch is `1/n_up = sqrt(1+gradient²)`. Footprint containment is polygon-based on every cell, not bounding-box overlap.

Actual view vectors come from camera centre−terrain point. Report azimuth clockwise from grid north, elevation above the local map plane, incidence `acos(n·view)`, and reciprocal positive cosine. The analytic composite-camera projection Jacobian gives two tangent sampling axes; these are geometric sampling proxies, **not image information/accuracy claims**. Backfacing cells are separately marked, not assigned useful sampling. No images are projected, matched or rectified.
