## Web/mobile boundary and provisional choices

Share **scientific meaning and conformance obligations**: identity, provenance, qualification, coordinate/vertical references, time/support, explicit failures and compatible read/query semantics. Share small pure application logic where actual hosts justify it. Rendering, touch/accessibility, permissions, file custody, background/lifecycle, ABI/library resources and recovery require platform-specific implementations and validation. Equivalent science need not mean identical runtimes or interfaces.

Use the already established web toolchain only as a **provisional reversible M1 reference host** if its local inventory and licences still permit it. This is an implementation starting point, not a final React/MapLibre/mobile-framework decision. Consult old lifecycle/camera behaviour individually; design a clean small interface, without copying the prototype hierarchy or migrating it by default. Confirm target access early within M1; do not restart a framework catalogue.

Reversal conditions: inability to supply genuine target-mobile interaction, terrain or local-resource behaviour; excessive measured target costs; licensing conflicts; failure of semantic conformance. Final renderer/framework, distributed package compatibility, app identifiers/signing and published contracts become expensive to reverse. Defer those commitments until their named evidence gates pass. No database, backend, cloud, universal shared core or extraction into unaudited private code is chosen here.

## Physical-device, cost, rights and privacy gates

Desktop reading, numerical conformance and isolated scientific replay are accepted. Emulation/portability can establish buildability or semantics; neither establishes field performance. Basic physical interaction belongs in the earliest available native prototype. Terrain frame-time distributions, memory, battery/thermal, touch/accessibility, interruptions/backgrounding, offline install/recovery and platform storage constraints belong before the corresponding mobile capability/suitability claim. Android evidence cannot stand in for iOS. Access/SDK/signing availability is unresolved, not proof the owner lacks hardware. No device was enumerated here. A complete device programme is not a prerequisite to writing the reversible web-reference M1 shell.

| Exposure | Earliest necessary controls |
|---|---|
| Private local development | Review dependencies and every actually used data/service route before use; visible attribution, bounded requests, no paid/new services by default. No location permission, tracking, analytics, accounts or personal-data collection needed for M1; declare remote map requests rather than promising no data exposure |
| Closed internal prototype | Pin supported hosts/resources; loading/failure/recovery scope, accessibility checks and licence notices; any paid/remote trial needs explicit bounded cost approval. No public endpoint implied |
| Controlled external testing | Exact redistribution/offline/binary rights, named supported devices/regions, consent where applicable, privacy/security and dependency review, update/recovery/support plan and bounded operating budget. Hashes alone do not authenticate a publisher |
| Unrestricted public exposure | F32 approved architecture/cost scenarios and enforceable safeguards, operational monitoring/suspension/recovery, public rights/brand/privacy/accessibility/security and maintenance readiness. A free hosted source is not an unlimited sustainable service |

Offline redistribution permission is required **before assembling/distributing the intended offline assets**, not inferred from an open renderer or downloadable data. No legal clearance or refreshed third-party terms is provided by this checkpoint. Future pricing should charge for genuine Meridian-created value or real provision costs rather than merely paywalling information free to provide; consider regional affordability without intrusive location-based pricing. Billing/monetisation infrastructure is deferred.
