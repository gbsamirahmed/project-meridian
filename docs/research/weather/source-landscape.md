# Weather source and model landscape

WR001, checked 10 October 2026. **PD** rows describe institutional products, not Meridian validation. Scientific relevance and practical access are separate columns. This is representative broad discovery; neither a procurement shortlist nor a model ranking. Exact current cycle, member count, grid, horizon and parameter availability must be pinned in later product-level work. [Rights register](source-access-rights.md), [dated sources](sources.md), [scientific landscape](scientific-landscape.md).

## Additional landscape and selection rules

Research AI systems (GraphCast, GenCast, Pangu, NeuralGCM), academic nowcasting, citizen/private sensors and specialist ocean/fire/space-weather products are identified in W35–W40 and the [taxonomy](capability-taxonomy.md). Their code, weights, training data and operational feeds can have different licences. No assumption of a supported free numerical feed follows from a paper or open code.

EI: candidate selection should specify region, variables, native intervals/levels, forecast leads, deterministic/ensemble need, matching independent observations, accessible retrospective forecasts and actual rights. A global baseline plus a regional candidate could be informative; not all regions need the same source. Consider provider changes, archive continuity and missing-cycle recovery before committing preparation effort. Neither resolution nor provider reputation is an accuracy oracle. Source availability and observation access are early gates, not reasons to narrow broad scientific discovery to GFS.

No dataset was fetched, credentials obtained, account opened or service trial performed. Formats/cadences/quotas are documentation findings where stated; actual API reliability and decode fidelity are untested. Provider specifications are deliberately not frozen in this discovery table.
