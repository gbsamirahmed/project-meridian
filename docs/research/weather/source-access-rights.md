# Preliminary Weather source access, rights and economics register

WR001, checked **10 October 2026**. Research assessment, **not legal clearance**. IDs refer to the [source landscape](source-landscape.md). Intended pathways are internal research, scientific processing and eventual application display/offline delivery; an A assessment applies only to the named documented product/path, not every institutional asset. Exact product terms and licence evidence must be preserved before acquisition and again before redistribution. [Sources/access limitations](sources.md).

**A** Usable under documented conditions. **B** Potentially usable with restrictions or further verification. **C** Requires paid access or commercial agreement. **D** Unsuitable for the specified intended use under identified restrictions. **E** Unresolved. D applies to a pathway, not a condemnation of the provider.

## Economics and service constraints

PD: ECMWF's inspected portal describes a **500 simultaneous-connection service limit**, not a client entitlement; full delivery can incur [service charges](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/DAC/pages/400498473/Service+Charges+From+01+July+2024). DataHub allowances are plan/product-specific; its FAQ distinguishes monthly allowance and daily usage, so do not divide one into an invented request-rate guarantee. Open-Meteo's free service conditions do not licence commercial endpoint use merely because the data are CC BY. MET Norway requires considerate traffic and can throttle; public NOAA delivery has no timeliness guarantee. Treat availability, rate limits and changes as source-adapter requirements, not promised uptime.

EI: source fees and Meridian's own costs must be budgeted separately. Drivers are fields × members × levels × times × area, retained cycles/archives, raw/prepared duplication, acquisition/decoding/regridding/calibration compute, request concurrency, storage/backups, logs, tile/chunk proliferation and end-user egress. Exact cost-per-experiment/download/user needs measured volume, provider tariff and realistic scenarios, not numbers invented here. [F32 operational gate](../../foundations/economics.md) still requires user-approved budget/exposure, controls, alerts/ownership and safe suspension; alerts are not hard caps. No infrastructure is justified by this research alone.

Preserve the product principle: no charging merely for information/functionality that costs Meridian nothing to provide; genuinely incurred licensed-data, processing, delivery and service costs may be recovered transparently. No commercial model designed.

## Required next rights evidence

Before a selected numerical experiment: identify exact source/product/version, licence URL/revision, attribution, native versus prepared distribution, commercial/internal permission, account/approval, format/field/interval coverage, retention/history, quota/concurrency and archive feasibility. Before offline/public delivery additionally verify modifications/derived notices, third-party rights, warning restrictions, retention/cache versus redistribution, geography, security/privacy and service agreement. Preserve an access-date record and qualified negative decisions. Consult provider/legal expertise when terms remain ambiguous; do not replace an unresolved term with an engineering assumption.
