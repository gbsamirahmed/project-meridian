## Executive conclusion

A global baseline and regional enhancement are credible **separate research tracks**. Three global families and two regional families warrant bounded attention; three surface-reference families offer different geographical and historical opportunities. No forecast has been shown superior here. Higher resolution, model agreement and public access establish neither skill nor decision suitability.

The clearest retrospective opportunity is **GFS archived operational forecasts and UKV archived forecasts assessed against the same MIDAS Open station observations**. It remains conditional on exact files, field metadata, QC, station exposure and height compatibility. Global validation in this proposed pilot means evaluating a globally applicable model in one region; it cannot establish worldwide skill. Swiss ICON products have documented current numerical access, but their public feed's short retention blocks an equivalent retrospective Alpine experiment unless a separate forecast archive is established. ECMWF's current open feeds are not themselves long-term forecast archives.

Three findings refine WR001 rather than rewrite it: UKV has an unsupported rolling two-year ASDI archive under a specifically documented open licence; MeteoSwiss's 24-hour retrieval window is not its forecast horizon; NOAA's ISD is superseded for current data. Rights, archive completeness and observational independence must be attached to **the delivery product and edition**, not just an institution or model name. See [detailed rights](source-rights-detail.md) and [comparison opportunities](forecast-reference-compatibility.md).

## Exact global products

### G1: IFS current subset versus historical forecast access

The [ECMWF open-data catalogue](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/open-data) documents regular 0.25-degree **GRIB2/CCSDS delivery**, four cycles at 00/06/12/18 UTC and a rolling last-12-run feed. For IFS control, 00/12 UTC lead times extend to 360 hours (three-hour steps through 144, then six-hour steps); 06/18 reach 144 hours. Ensemble products are separately identified. Surface fields include 2 m temperature, 10 m wind components, precipitation and interval gust diagnostics; selected pressure levels are available. Exact member identifiers/count, vertical support and every field's interval must be read from the selected edition rather than borrowed from a full-resolution service.

The [technical dissemination guide](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/DAC/pages/272310539/ECMWF+open+data+real-time+forecasts+from+IFS+and+AIFS) is the reference for stream/type/step/product addressing. An internal model grid or vertical coordinate is **not** the regular delivery grid or supplied pressure-level representation. Full native vertical configuration for a future selected experiment remains UNRESOLVED here. Release schedules are not immutable guarantees of issue time: retain cycle reference, actual release/retrieval and valid time separately.

For history, [TIGGE's catalogue](https://ecds.ecmwf.int/datasets/tigge-forecasts?tab=overview) offers archived forecasts from multiple centres with selectable control/perturbed products, not reanalysis. Its [download form](https://ecds.ecmwf.int/datasets/tigge-forecasts?tab=download) requires login/licence acceptance. Catalogue-wide history/options do not establish that a particular ECMWF variable, member, grid, lead and month all exist. **Exact contemporary TIGGE redistribution terms and authorised access remain unresolved**, as do unrestricted MARS entitlements. Current open-data rights cannot be substituted for these historical channels. IFS cycle changes also require a run-level version record; a current catalogue update is not the version of a 2025 forecast.

### G2: GFS 0.25-degree historical operational product

[GDEX d084001, DOI 10.5065/D65D8PWK](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d084001/) identifies operational GFS analysis and forecasts from 15 January 2015, GRIB2 on a 1440 × 721 regular grid, four UTC cycles, three-hour leads to 240 hours and twelve-hour sampling thereafter to 384. This is an **archive specification**, not the hourly sampling of Meridian's retained experiment. The selected +24/+48 forecasts are distinct from initial analyses. The product record assigns CC BY 4.0; archive services and original producer conditions remain separate.

The [NOAA GFS NODD registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-gfs-bdp-pds/) documents anonymous delivery and NOAA conditions but contains dated model-resolution/vertical descriptions. These are not used as current operational specifications. The [producer system page](https://www.emc.ncep.noaa.gov/emc/pages/numerical_forecast_systems/gfs.php) identifies the system; precise current model version, full vertical coordinates and cycle-specific field inventory remain UNKNOWN within this documentary record. Near-surface temperature must be confirmed as the GRIB parameter at 2 m above model ground, in K, with instantaneous time support, before acquisition/interpretation is authorised.

An archive inconsistency prevents a claim of current completeness: GDEX's page says updates would stop in early 2026, lists conflicting rolling/persistent source descriptions, and returned an end date later than this review date. Completed historical year listings support investigating January 2025, **not** a guarantee of gap-free files or current retention. Archive authentication/subset-service requirements could not be completely verified. The [NOAA range-download guide](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/wesley/fast_downloading_grib.html) documents indexed extraction as an option; no range request was made. Pin exact index/message identities, packing, surface support and model orography if used later.

### G3: AIFS operational products and versioned history

[ECMWF AIFS documentation](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/aifs-machine-learning-data) separates Single and ENS. Single became operational in February 2025 and ENS in July 2025; v2 replaced earlier versions in May 2026. It describes six-hour outputs to 15 days, four daily cycles and native N320 output; an open regular-grid product is a different representation. Both use ECMWF operational analyses for initialisation. Surface/upper-air field lists, selected member count and all diagnostic meanings need the exact delivery edition; they are not inherited wholesale from IFS.

The page describes Single archive availability from February 2024, including experimental products, and ENS from July 2025. MARS history is access-controlled; free unrestricted retrospective delivery is **not established**. [Single v2 implementation](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/FCST/pages/620418870/Implementation+of+AIFS+Single+v2) documents a change in initialisation compatibility with IFS Cycle 50r1. A cross-version study must partition those changes. Training/tuning overlap with a proposed reference period is unresolved. AIFS versus IFS could investigate differences under shared initialisation, not independent confirmation of physical truth. No ML skill ranking is inferred.

## Exact regional products

### R1: UKV ASDI, not a generic Met Office API

The [specific UKV registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/met-office-uk-deterministic/) describes an unsupported rolling **two-year** NetCDF archive with a 3–6 hour delivery delay. Short runs at 00/06/09/12/18/21 UTC reach +54 hours; medium runs at 03/15 reach +120. Additional nowcast cycles are separate. This is a delivery product, not all native UKV fields. Its anonymous public distribution carries CC BY-SA 4.0; [current provider channel guidance](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/external-data-channels) links the ASDI product and its datasheet.

The [PS47 ASDI datasheet](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/api/assets/file/ukv-2km-ps47-asdi-pdf-updatespdf?prefix=assets) distinguishes pre/post late-January-2026 representations, including numerical precision and level changes. `temperature_at_screen_level` is instantaneous K at **1.5 m**; maximum/minimum fields are different quantities. Retain the actual historical file's attributes, coordinates, missing values and orography, not current PS47 assumptions. Selected surface temperature does not require all model levels. Delivered 2 km/0.018-degree spacing, native model grid and resampled coordinates must remain distinct.

Exact current domain polygon, coordinate arrays, historical model version, hybrid-level definition, issue timestamp and candidate files have not been inspected. Domain membership must be established from delivered coordinates/support, not a UK label or an old bounding box. The [older planetary-scale page](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/planetary-scale-applications-from-the-uk-met-office) describes an older, differently licensed service. This conflict is explicitly retained in the rights report. DataHub GRIB, blended point forecasts and ASDI NetCDF are separate channels with different agreements; DataHub restrictions are not automatically ASDI restrictions. UKV's driving global model/boundary cycle for the selected historical edition remains UNKNOWN here.

### R2: ICON-CH1/CH2-EPS native OGD

The [MeteoSwiss numerical-product guide](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/e-forecast-data/e2-e3-numerical-weather-forecasting-model) documents CH1 (~1 km, 11 members, three-hour cycles, 33-hour forecasts) and CH2 (~2.1 km, 21 members, six-hour cycles, 120-hour forecasts), hourly GRIB2 output, 80 full/81 half terrain-following levels and approximate publication latency. The **24-hour access window follows publication**, not the valid forecast horizon. Coordinate/static files, grid UUIDs, height definitions and local parameter tables are essential. Static assets are not versioned; retain their exact identity with each cycle. Boundary cells may contain unsuitable values: precise usable-domain support is a prerequisite, not a rectangular extent assumption.

The [2026 operational ICON paper](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/19/755/2026/) concerns version 2024.10, reports Alpine domains, KENDA initialisation and ECMWF IFS ENS lateral boundaries, and describes a longer specific CH1 cycle. That differs from the current feed guide's uniform summary. Do not transfer the paper's exceptional horizon or point count into an assumed current feed. [Product changes](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/de/e-forecast-data/e2-e3-numerical-weather-forecasting-model-changelog) require an edition/cycle pin. The model system and public product are related but not interchangeable specifications.

Commercial/derived/offline use has a documented CC BY 4.0 basis under [OGD terms](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/general/terms-of-use), subject to attribution, restrictions and upstream service conditions. A long-term public forecast archive has not been established. KENDA analysis, ICON reanalysis and current forecasts cannot fill that gap as if they were historical operational forecasts. No demonstrated Swiss-versus-global skill result is claimed by Meridian.

Reference time/initialisation, issue/release, valid time and retrieval are distinct even when a catalogue supplies only some. **Regional skill evidence:** the ICON paper describes operational verification against the preceding COSMO system, not a controlled UKV/GFS or IFS/ICON comparison for the proposed reference cohort. A relevant published UKV-versus-global temperature comparison for the selected station/lead/period profile was not established here. Model orography is an explanatory covariate, not proof of greater skill. Any enhancement claim remains a later validation question, including cases where a global product performs better.

## Feasibility outcome and programme changes

**Global baseline feasibility: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED documentary opportunity.** Global operational products and several history routes are documented. GFS plus a qualified UK station edition offers a bounded semantic/verification-design route. Worldwide observational representativeness and production sustainability remain unresolved.

**Regional enhancement feasibility: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED for UK retrospective comparison; Alpine retrospective opportunity BLOCKED at archive/domain-version closure.** Neither implies enhancement has been empirically demonstrated. Regional products complement global context; no substitution, blending or safety inference follows.

Proceed to quantity/reference semantics and a preregistered verification design, while treating archive/rights checks as explicit acquisition gates. This is a refinement of W10/W11/W30/W36/W38/W40, not completion of those broad disciplines. Preserve WR001 statuses and add bounded documentary findings in the [register](research-register.md). Prioritise station exposure/height, catalogue version drift, shared assimilation and rolling archive loss over additional provider enumeration. Source licences and delivery-service terms remain parallel gates; not every open question requires another landscape survey.

Exactly one next bounded task is defined in the [compatibility report](forecast-reference-compatibility.md#exactly-one-next-bounded-task): **WEATHER RESEARCH 003 — NEAR-SURFACE TEMPERATURE SEMANTICS AND FORECAST–REFERENCE VERIFICATION DESIGN — NOT BEGUN**.
