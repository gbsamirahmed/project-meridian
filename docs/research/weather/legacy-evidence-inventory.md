# Independent assessment of legacy Meridian Weather evidence

WR001, 10 October 2026. **Ordering:** primary external science/access research and the independent landscape/taxonomy were established before this detailed code review. The inventory maps legacy work against them; it does not define Weather's scope. Starting public checkpoint `6944bcd444c9645191f815c6eb6f2e78fe2e7f83`. Files/publications were inspected read-only, not regenerated. [Programme](research-programme.md), [semantic obligations](scientific-landscape.md#scientific-data-semantics--research-obligations-not-a-schema).

Classification is per claim: **useful scientific evidence** (bounded native decoding/meaning/receipt), **potentially reusable engineering**, **historical implementation**, **unvalidated assumption**, **known limitation**, **superseded experiment**, **requires further investigation**. Existing tests are inspected evidence, **not tests rerun by WR001**. Engineering validation is not forecast-skill validation.

## Retained publications and validation evidence

Existing `${MERIDIAN_DATA_ROOT}/derived/weather/gfs` holds `20260907T12Z`, `20260907T18Z` and `latest.json`, plus retained validation material. Current catalogue selects the 18Z run, ten fields and +1…+24 h. Read-only preservation inventory: **40,842 files, 1,275,692,110 bytes**; all hashes compared with the previous accepted inventory. The retirement manifest's **40,841 files, 1,268,050,554 bytes** describes an earlier dataset-only inventory; the additional validation material explains the different scope, not new research acquisition. No new forecast run/publication was created. The older derived-route report studied a different September run; its measurements are not silently attributed to these two runs.

Inspected tests: [GFS builder](../../../scripts/weather/test_gfs_weather_builder.py) covers native metadata, accumulation planning, zero/missing, encodings and antimeridian handling; [atmospheric tests](../../../scripts/weather/test_gfs_atmospheric.py) cover level/time selection, sentinel-before-interpolation, ranges/quantisation and failed-publication preservation; [updater tests](../../../scripts/weather/test_gfs_updater.py) cover incomplete/mixed/schema runs, staging/recovery, locking, pointer failures and retention; [publication tests](../../../scripts/weather/test_gfs_publication.mjs) cover path/root/selected-run and materialisation behaviour. [Refresh](../../../scripts/weather/test_catalogue_refresh.mjs), [scalar](../../../scripts/weather/test_scalar_rendering.mjs), [pressure](../../../scripts/weather/test_pressure_contours.mjs), [temperature](../../../scripts/weather/test_temperature_contours.mjs), [local atmosphere](../../../scripts/weather/test_local_atmosphere.mjs) and [route atmosphere](../../../scripts/route/test_atmospheric_conditions.mjs) provide historical contract/display cases. WR001 did not rerun them or infer new pass counts.

Useful evidence: native product identification, scientifically important time/vertical/missingness distinctions and bounded preparation/publication tests. **Not established:** independent observed forecast verification, regional/seasonal skill, calibrated probabilities, model selection, reliable local summit/route conditions, mobile resource suitability, complete offline installation, long-term forecast archive, production rights or operational delivery. Two retained cycles cannot establish these claims. The independent programme may retain, revise or reject individual algorithms only after scoped evidence; no legacy component was deleted, modified or promoted into a requirement.
