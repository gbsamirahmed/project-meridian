## Findings and high-impact questions

Broad discovery adds assimilation/reference dependence, ensemble calibration, ML/hybrid prediction and distribution shift, radar/satellite retrieval and nowcasting, coupled land/snow/radiation, composition, marine/hydrometeorology, fire, seasonal/climate and space-weather questions. These are identified topics, **not commitments to implement every capability**. Specialist impacts and rare hazards need additional expertise and validation.

Source access is not one free/paid label. Documented open pathways exist at ECMWF, MeteoSwiss, ECCC and NOAA; exact products, exceptions and service conditions remain important. Met Office DataHub distribution restrictions, Open-Meteo's non-commercial free endpoint versus CC BY data, MET Norway traffic/privacy rules and product-dependent EUMETSAT terms can affect architecture. Public websites, academic papers, model code and operational numerical feeds are different assets. No redistribution clearance or pricing guarantee is claimed.

Unresolved priorities are lawful retrospective forecast/reference availability; station QC/exposure/elevation and assimilation independence; accumulation/vertical/vector semantics; comparable ensemble/probability support; complex-terrain representativeness; verification baselines/sample power; warning authority/expiry; and the resource cost of fields × levels × members × times × regions × versions. Rights and reference suitability should precede selecting a numerical experiment; semantic and verification design can run alongside them. No numerical benchmark, regional forecast quality or hazard suitability was established here.

Legacy work contains useful source-meaning/decoder and publication/recovery engineering evidence. It remains limited to a finite GFS experiment and simplified point-provider adapter; two retained cycles, quantised/resampled tiles, rendered contours and route display policies do not establish calibrated or local skill. Its 64 MiB cache target is not a hard reader/GPU limit. Current model-based API output is not automatically an observation. No code was changed.
