# Weather capability taxonomy

WR001, 10 October 2026. Broad discovery, not a feature commitment. **All families below are identified/scoped research topics, not Meridian-validated capabilities.** [Landscape](scientific-landscape.md) defines scientific and validation distinctions; [register](research-register.md) tracks investigations. Sources are the WMO observation/quantity conventions, CF conventions and operational product catalogues linked there and in [source landscape](source-landscape.md). Source-specific definitions always override shorthand examples.

Roles: **O** observation; **M** model variable; **D** diagnostic/derived scientific product; **H** heuristic/impact indicator. One quantity may have several roles: name the actual product and method. Units below are representative conventions, not a mandate to convert every source. Scales describe interpretation requirements, not universal forecast availability.

|---|---|---|
| C01 Air and surface temperature — W01/W12 | O/M air K or °C at named height/level; M skin/soil temperatures distinct | Instant/mean/min/max interval and model-ground elevation; communicate level/source/age, investigate diurnal and altitude representativeness |
| C02 Pressure and upper-air structure — W02/W12 | O/M surface pressure Pa/hPa; D sea-level pressure; M geopotential m²/s² or height m on pressure levels | Sea-level reduction not summit pressure; below-ground levels and synoptic support explicit; validate conversions/vertical definitions |

| C16 Wind chill/heat stress — W01/W21 | D apparent temperature °C; H indices, WBGT °C or named index, UTCI °C | Empirical validity, activity/exposure/clothing/solar assumptions differ; communicate method/limits, not medical or safety conclusion |
| C17 Air quality/aerosols/pollen — W09/W22 | O/M concentration µg/m³, gases source-specific mixing ratio, optical depth; D jurisdictional AQ index | Chemistry/emissions/deposition and averaging, index standards differ; independent composition validation/rights track, optional relevance |
| C18 Surface conditions — W06/W23 | O/M/D soil temperature/moisture, surface fluxes, wetness/frost/snow state; H walking conditions | Model land surface not actual path; standing water, mud, grip or stream crossings require other evidence; reject physical absence/safety inference |

| C20 Warnings/impact guidance — W25 | Official advisory/CAP/text with issuing authority, event/severity/urgency/certainty/area and time | Warning thresholds/content/cancellation have jurisdictional meaning; source identity intact; no paraphrase that alters advice, links before replication |
| C21 Marine/coastal weather — W26 | O/M/D wave height m, period s, direction conventions, coastal winds/surge | Wind versus swell and tidal/vertical references; coupled domains, jurisdiction/rights; later coastal relevance, not flood/navigation guarantee |

| C25 Space weather and additional coupled topics — W29 | O/M solar/geomagnetic/ionospheric products; weather-adjacent rather than tropospheric fields | Potential positioning/communications relevance; discovery retained but deep work deferred, no astronomy renderer or new product requirement |

## Interpretation rules and breadth

This taxonomy includes every requested principal variable, plus hydrometeorology, marine/coastal coupling, fire weather, aerosols/pollen, climate context and space-weather relevance. Inclusion does not imply implementation or an initial map layer. Cloud microphysics, model physics and sampling underpin several families and are not duplicated as UI categories.

A consumer must be able to identify **quantity, role, source/model, support/level, time/interval, method, missingness and principal limitation**. Temperature colours, moving particles, cloud opacity and warnings are different presentations, not different scientific authorities. Native categorical labels and probability event definitions survive preparation. Unknown is not zero or physical absence. Forecast age is not validity proof. No single common confidence score is justified across these families.

Prioritise W10/W11/W30/W31 (rights/semantics/verification/provenance) before proving a selected numerical family. Wind, precipitation and temperature are plausible first validation subjects because they expose vectors, intervals and terrain representativeness; not because the legacy pipeline implements them. A source-feasibility task should decide whether ensembles/observations can support a better first experiment. High-consequence C14–C16/C20 require separate expertise/verification; C18 never implies route or avalanche suitability.
