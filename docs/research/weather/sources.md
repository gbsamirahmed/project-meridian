## Coverage limits and evidence handling

Some pages were fully retrieved, others only primary indexed extracts; explicit failures above bound claims. Search-engine publication/crawl labels were not treated as document dates. Institutional help pages and dated operational announcements can differ because services change; selected current product terms must be captured and discrepancies resolved before use. Access failures do not prove a service is scientifically unusable or data unavailable. No secondary architecture/marketing commentary supplies scientific validation here.

WR001 establishes an independent discovery-level map and preliminary documentary access findings. It does not exhaust all national agencies, textbooks, instrument uncertainty budgets, scientific literature, quotas, regional archives or licences. Air quality, hydrology, fire, marine and space weather are identified beyond immediate scope. Later studies should preserve exact source/table/version/quote location and only claim the depth they actually completed. General physical principles and proposed experiments are not new Meridian scientific results.
