## Status vocabulary and precedence

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CLOSED | The bounded investigation finished, including negative/inconclusive outcomes. Does not mean the wider scientific problem is solved. |
| CLOSED FOR FOUNDATION | Sufficient evidence/declaration exists for the stated foundation. Reopen only on a demonstrated contradiction; implementation or coverage gaps alone do not qualify. |
| PARTIAL | Useful results exist, but the specified wider claim is not established. This is not an active experiment instruction. |
| OPEN | An unresolved question has a current justified role; no automatic execution or production adoption. |
| PARKED | Relevant work deliberately awaits a stated dependency or later priority. |
| BLOCKED | A specific required external input prevents an authorized bounded proof. Here the Swiss provisioning dependency is recorded under PARKED, rather than duplicating labels. |
| SUPERSEDED | An earlier abstraction, proposed prerequisite or next step was replaced/completed by later evidence. Surviving portions are identified separately. |
| OPTIONAL / ADVANCED | Relevant later work, not required before the present synthesis gate. Often literature-backed but untested locally. |

Each status applies to the **named question**, not the entire field. A successful proof is separately identified from an implemented production capability. A closed experiment can leave a PARTIAL general problem. A rejected control is a result, not missing work to repeat.

For present sequencing, use this dated register and the programme below. Frozen contracts remain authoritative for their own invariants. Detailed reports remain authoritative for methods/results. Dated development entries and old “next” sections are historical; they neither override later closures nor authorize new work. Product direction supplies broader hypotheses, not an alternative Atlas task queue.
