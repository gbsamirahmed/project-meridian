## 1. Executive decision

**C - READY FOR BOUNDED REGIONAL PILOT.** The retained proofs cover the foundational
behaviours needed to begin design/build of a **local retained Tryfan integrated persistent
pilot**. All nine admission gates pass within that boundary. No concrete foundational blocker
or mandatory pre-pilot research prerequisite is demonstrated. This is a programme admission
decision, not acceptance of an already implemented pilot, public delivery or production architecture.

Terrain/lifecycle, categorical semantics, appearance qualification and Exe time/feature/reference
proofs are materially different. Another region or water/cover dataset would repeat known classes
without exposing a specific missing foundation. The missing work is integration: a coherent mixed
catalogue, publication and query/asset read boundary, measured as one finite regional workload.
Those are pilot exit obligations, not reasons to accumulate more abstract/domain proofs.

Appearance remains unfinished. The pilot can preserve ordinary dated source-derived imagery and
its limitations while correction, intrinsic appearance, relighting and multiview remain separate.
No successful normalization is implied by INCONCLUSIVE or NOT JUSTIFIED trials. Exactly one next
task is defined in section36 and has not begun.

## 2. Acceptance question

Can heterogeneous representations, qualified evidence, exact provenance, time/reference roles,
scoped derivations, revision history, persistence and queries compose without a known foundational
mistake? For each unresolved question ask: **could a different answer realistically require a
foundational change to the persistent model?** A new adapter/method, richer quality, rendering
improvement or increased capacity normally does not. A compulsory semantic conflation would.

The answer applies to the selected pilot workload. It is not a proof of universal extensibility,
complete Earth knowledge, scientific accuracy or every future domain. Excluded behaviours are not
silently graded as proven. The manifest records explicit limits and unproven classes.

## 8. Terrain readiness

Terrain foundations are closed. Welsh source/pyramid plus existing selector proves second-region
portability; common-only/refined Tryfan results exercise support-driven selection in world-model
queries. Swiss parents and terminal controls establish that LOD, source handoff, morphology,
continuity and accuracy are different. The pilot must use accepted unchanged products and disclose
unsupported regions/unknown vertical lineage, not revive rejected seam algorithms.

Regional activation/read-path acceptance is engineering. It does not require harmonising unknown
AWS heights with Welsh or Swiss heights into one bare-earth truth. Derived values concern an explicitly
qualified represented-heightfield quantity. H8/T8 do not demonstrate a mandatory true3D remedy.
No source selection or terrain method research is reopened.

## 9. Multiscale readiness

Closed M1/M3 foundations distinguish source sampling, information scale, representation levels,
analysis scale, directional display sampling and steep tangent-surface sampling. M2's negative
