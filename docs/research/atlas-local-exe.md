## 7. Water semantics

WFD is mapped/reference membership, not instantaneous water presence. RFO is an
accepted historical event outline, not necessarily simultaneous inundation at
all points and not a forecast. GSW is observation-derived classification:
**0 = no-observation,1 = non-detection,2 = detection**. Neither code1 nor missing
records establishes physical absence. Code0 is a retained observation gap, not a
missing file or evidence that the cell was dry.

Native ecological/chemical attributes, Heavily Modified designation, boundary
convention, flood event associations and source classification limitations remain
native metadata. They are not converted into a universal water value or ranked
against each other. The centre-count summaries are derived code compositions,
not calibrated confidence, water fractions or physical transitions.
